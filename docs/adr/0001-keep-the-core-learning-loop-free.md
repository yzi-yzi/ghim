# Keep the Core Learning Loop free

Ghim will permanently keep Capture, useful Learning Material within a transparent fair-use allowance, due Daily Reviews, personal decks, basic progress features, and Learner Data export in the Free Core. Recurring revenue will come from one Ghim Pro subscription selling scale, convenience, and personalization—not better memory, access to existing data, advertising, or sale of Learner Data—because a long-term learning product depends on continuity and trust even though enrichment, sync, storage, and audio create recurring costs.

## Consequences

Capture and due reviews remain available when quotas or provider budgets are exhausted; affected captures become Enrichment Pending. Free launches provisionally with 300 Successful Enrichments per calendar month, while private beta measures actual cost and willingness to pay before Ghim Pro pricing or checkout is introduced.
